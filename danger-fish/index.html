Frequently asked questions

How do I increase my score?

Collect starfish as you swim through the reef. Each starfish adds to your score.

Why does my fish become smaller?

Colliding with coral or dangerous jellyfish causes your fish to shrink. As your fish becomes smaller, staying in control becomes increasingly challenging.

How do I restore my fish's size?

Collect fish food to help your fish regain its size and continue swimming.

What does the blue jellyfish do?

The blue jellyfish steals points from your score, so avoid it whenever possible.

How do I control the fish?

Tap the screen to help your fish swim and stay clear of hazards.

Does Danger Fish require an internet connection?

An internet connection may be required to load advertising. Core gameplay remains available when an advertisement is not available.
