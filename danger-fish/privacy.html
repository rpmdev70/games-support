Privacy policy

Danger Fish

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains how information may be processed when you use Danger Fish.

Danger Fish does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address or other direct contact information in order to play.

Information processed by the app

The game may store information locally on your device, such as game settings, scores or gameplay progress. This information is used to operate the game and is not directly collected by RPM Games unless you choose to provide it when contacting support.

Advertising

Danger Fish uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising interactions and diagnostic information.

The information processed and the ads shown may depend on your device settings, region and consent choices.

For more information about how Google processes information, please review Google's privacy policy.

Google Privacy Policy

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Danger Fish uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present privacy information and request or manage advertising consent.

Depending on your location, you may be able to review or change your advertising privacy choices from within the game or through your device settings.

Children's privacy

Danger Fish is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through a support request may contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

Support communications

If you contact support, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. This information is used only to respond to your enquiry and manage related support.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
