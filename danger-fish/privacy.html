Privacy policy

Danger Fish

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains how information may be processed when you use Danger Fish.

Danger Fish does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address or other direct contact information in order to play.

Locally stored game information

Danger Fish may store information locally on your device, including game settings, scores and gameplay progress. This information is used to operate the game and is not directly collected by RPM Games unless you choose to provide it when contacting support.

Advertising

Danger Fish uses Google AdMob to display third-party advertising.

Danger Fish is configured to use limited advertising only. It does not use advertising data to personalise advertisements based on your interests, previous activity or advertising profile.

Limited ads disable advertising personalisation and features that require the use of local advertising identifiers. Google may still process limited technical information required to deliver and operate advertising, including IP address information. Because programmatic limited ads are enabled, Google may also use limited device storage for fraud and invalid traffic prevention.

Danger Fish does not use Google AdMob for remarketing, interest-based targeting or personalised advertising.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please review:

Google Privacy Policy

Privacy choices and regional requirements

Danger Fish does not use personalised advertising, interest-based advertising profiles or remarketing.

Privacy and advertising requirements may vary by country or region. Where required by applicable law, additional privacy information or choices may be provided.

You can also manage general privacy and tracking permissions through the privacy settings provided by your device and operating system.

Children's privacy

Danger Fish is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

Danger Fish does not require users to create an account or directly provide personal information in order to play.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through a support request may contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

Support communications

If you contact support, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. This information is used only to respond to your enquiry and manage related support.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
